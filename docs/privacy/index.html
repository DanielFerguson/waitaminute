WaitAMinute privacy

Effective 13 August 2026

WaitAMinute checks the hostname of pages you visit so it can apply the block rules you choose. It does not read page content, form entries, passwords, cookies, or browsing history.

Your rules and settings are stored using Chrome Sync when you have enabled Chrome Sync. Local statistics record blocked attempts and completed soft-block challenges by hostname. Temporary bypasses exist only for the current browser session.

WaitAMinute does not operate an analytics service, send your data to the developer, sell data, use data for advertising, or permit humans to read your browsing activity. Data is used only to provide the extension's blocking and statistics features.

Contact: WaitAMinute support.
